# Tour20: timed Cartesian bead in the complete generated tour

The opt-in timed Cartesian pose objective was applied to the previously generated Tour20 bead `weld:5` on its original modeled clock. Its generated endpoint q/qd/qdd stayed fixed; both adjacent quintic tangent leads, every generated probe, transit and fixture stage were retained. One scene served optimization and the complete independent verifier. The [actual-curve evidence](tour20-timed-pose-actual-evidence.json) contains all stage, join, source-process, collision, position and speed results.

The optimizer reported convergence in 0.026 s under contention. Its common research settings were timed-pose weight 1, process weight 10 and a native mixed-axis initial trust box of 0.01, matching the previous three-block study. The coalescing rule removed no knots. The bead was already nearly ideal: sampled feed stayed approximately 4.999964 mm/s, maximum timed target position departure was approximately 5e-12 m and normalized integrated squared jerk was 1.42e-14 s⁻³. Baseline and trial were effectively identical; this is an integration check, not a demonstrated improvement.

The verifier reconstructed all 20 actual stages and sampled 1,025 states per stage, finding no penetrating contacts. Numerical position-extrema and sampled sourced-speed checks passed. All 19 modeled q/qd/qdd joins were present and the greatest absolute residuals were approximately 1.4e-15, 8.6e-14 and 1e-11 in their respective native axis and modeled-time units; no joint-linear process corners were reported. Total modeled duration remained 44.02 s. These are finite-sample checks, not a continuous-clearance or physical execution proof. The probe hit is assumed at the commanded limit, fixture and process timing is modeled, the outer physical derivatives are unknown and generated leads and manufacturing tolerances remain unapproved.

The first trial checked all stages but then failed its scene-state guard because the fixture-aware group construction left the shared environment at the bead fixture state. The opt-in path now saves and restores the state and revision in a `finally` context; a focused test exercises successful and failing operations. The rerun above passed the guard. Its timing overlapped other work and must not be used as a clean speed benchmark.

Using the research environment and explicit saved inputs described in [README.md](README.md), run `actual_tour_trial.py` with `--timed-pose-bead`, the fresh Tour20 candidate, cruise-probe batch, generated tangent-lead and connected-weld evidence, and `--weld-stage weld:5`. This one-bead adapter is not a Tour09 or all-tour implementation: the existing multi-cluster verifier requires a stage-local actual-curve adapter before those clusters can be optimized and reconstructed without altering their source/blend targets or their existing leads.
