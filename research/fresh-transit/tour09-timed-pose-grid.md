# Tour09: timed pose and generated weld curves

This opt-in research trial refines the previously generated Tour09 weld clusters using the same stage-local cubic, modeled-clock, process and collision formulation as the Tour16 experiment. The verified generated probes, fixture curves and transits are retained. Workpiece-relative position and orientation targets come from the existing generated blend; no recorded intermediate robot joints are used. The [compact evidence](tour09-timed-pose-grid-evidence.json) contains all local checks, raw boundary data, the original baseline and the incomplete full-tour attempt.

A uniform 5% sample-spacing ablation was previewed before optimization. It removed one ordinary generated sample next to a retained blend transition in cluster 1202; no samples were removed in the other two clusters. The largest sampled FK change caused by that refit was 1.5e-8 m and 1.4e-8 rad. The same rule removed the same samples as the original 0.1% rule in the three previously studied Tour16 blocks. The rule is exploratory; it is not a process tolerance or proof that the curves are identical.

With pose weight 1, all three Tour09 cluster SQPs reported convergence after the broader coalescing. Their local 1,025-state collision checks and numerical position and speed checks passed, but the full verifier stopped after 16 of 67 stages because a source Cartesian anchor differed from the existing reconstruction band. In addition, process quality materially regressed: a generated lead's maximum timed-target departure rose from 0.081 to 5.48 mm and one bead's from 0.026 to 0.470 mm. The original generated Tour09 remains the checked baseline.

The first failing endpoint had an actual 1.85e-7 m source-anchor residual. The endpoint and workpiece frame were correct and hard endpoint line/progress rows were present; their accepted numerical residuals were within the solver's existing 1e-4 feasibility band. Its physical significance is unestablished. Another source endpoint shifted 0.0527 mm. Neither this failure nor local convergence is a manufacturing acceptance decision.

The same scene then compared pose weights 10 and 100 with two window policies on all three clusters. Full-cluster optimization failed SQP on cluster 1202 at both weights. Bead-only optimization retained the existing generated leads and their modeled q/qd/qdd boundaries and converged locally on all clusters; the two short straight beads stayed close to their existing ~0.004 mm target error and ~5 mm/s feed. The four-segment bead still reached a 0.266 mm timed-target departure at weight 100 versus 0.026 mm baseline, with increased jerk on short segments. These are local diagnostics, not a selected profile or a full-tour result. The original leads in this Tour09 batch are the already generated cubic curves; they were not replaced by quintic fits.

The matched first-QP PIQP scaling replay is documented separately in [the replay note](../qp-scale-replay/README.md). All timings are contended correctness observations. Actual controller timing, physical outer derivatives, process tolerances, fixture dynamics and collision clearance between samples remain unknown.
